About Cluck & Co.

Cluck & Co. is a family-run poultry farm dedicated to raising healthy, happy chickens. We believe that quality starts with proper care, clean coops, and good nutrition.

Our chickens are raised in a stress-free environment with plenty of space to roam. Each bird is vaccinated, health-checked, and ready to join your flock or supply your table.

We specialize in broiler and layer chickens of various breeds and ages, sold at fair prices in Philippine pesos. Whether you're a backyard hobbyist or a commercial buyer, we're here to help.
